Privacy Policy

How Awaken Coffee Bar handles personal information collected through this website. Last updated August 2026.

Placeholder wording for the demo. Awaken Coffee Bar should have this policy reviewed against the Privacy Act 2020 and the tools actually in use before the site goes live.

1. Who we are

Awaken Coffee Bar, 28 MacDonald Street, Mount Maunganui 3116, Bay of Plenty, New Zealand. We are the agency responsible for personal information collected through this website under the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

This website has no contact form and no sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address or phone number through this site. Information only reaches us when you choose to contact us:

  • If you call us on 022 412 1741, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and the content of your message.
  • Your web browser sends standard technical information to whoever hosts this site, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you viewed.

3. Why we hold it

We use what you send us to answer your question, take an order over the phone or sort out a problem. We do not sell personal information and we do not add you to a marketing list without asking.

4. Third party services on this site

Some parts of this site are provided by other companies, and using them means information passes to them under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps. The map near the bottom of the homepage is embedded from Google and loads when you view that section.
  • Google Fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google's font service.
  • Google reviews. The review and leave-a-review buttons take you to Google, where Google's terms and privacy policy apply.
  • Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Social links open those platforms in a new tab.
  • Email. The email buttons open a compose window in your own email service. Nothing is sent until you send it.

5. Cookies

This site does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Embedded services such as Google Maps may set cookies of their own. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, though embedded content may stop working properly if you do.

6. How long we keep information

Emails and phone messages are kept only as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and to meet any record keeping we are legally required to do, then they are deleted.

7. Storage and security

Email is held in our email provider's systems, which may store data on servers outside New Zealand. We take reasonable steps to keep information secure, but no method of transmitting information over the internet is completely secure.

8. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask for a copy, and ask us to correct it if it is wrong. Contact us and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

9. Children

This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them through it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on this page is the one that applies.

11. Contact and complaints

Privacy questions can go to Awaken Coffee Bar, 28 MacDonald Street, Mount Maunganui 3116, or by phone on 022 412 1741. If you are not happy with how we handle a privacy matter you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.
