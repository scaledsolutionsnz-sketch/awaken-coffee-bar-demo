What we make

What we make

Coffee first. Then the food that actually keeps you going.

Everything on the counter is made to be eaten on the way somewhere, before a session at Flex or on the drive down Maunganui Road. Small counter, short list, nothing sitting around all day.

The main event

Flat whites and espresso

The one people come back for. Strong, creamy, poured properly, and made the same way every single morning whether you are first in at 6am or last at midday.

Warm days

Iced coffee and cold drinks

Iced lattes, iced long blacks and a proper spread of cold options for the walk back to the beach. Good variety, not just one sad bottle in a fridge.

Post session

Smoothies

Blended to order and built to sit alongside a workout rather than undo it. Handy when you want something in you but do not feel like a full feed.

House made

Protein balls and high-protein treats

Rolled here, not shipped in. These are the ones people mention in their reviews, so grab a couple while they are still on the counter.

Real food

Quiche, toasties and wedges

Hot, savoury and gone quickly. The wedges in particular tend to sell out, so an early start is your best bet if you have your heart set on them.

Sweet side

Muffins, cheesecake and baking

Blackforest muffins, cheesecake and whatever else came out of the oven that morning. Baked here, so the list shifts a little week to week.

About Awaken

A small coffee bar the locals found before the tourists did.

Awaken sits inside Flex Fitness on MacDonald Street, which is exactly why people call it a hidden gem. It is not on the main strip and it is not trying to be a full cafe. It is a proper coffee bar with someone behind the machine who knows what they are doing and remembers how you take it.

The doors open at 6am on weekdays for the early crew, the gym members and anyone heading to work. The food is made here, the coffee is made to order, and the chat comes free.

  • Open from 6am weekdays. Sorted well before most places in the Mount have turned the machine on.
  • Food made in-house. Quiche, protein balls, muffins and cheesecake, not a supplier box opened out the back.
  • Five stars across 64 Google reviews. Every one of them left by someone who actually walked through the door.
